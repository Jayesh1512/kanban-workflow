# Antseed introduction deck

Updated 15 November 2026. Eleven slides: introduction, problem, why now, P2P network, operating flow, differentiation, trust and verification, who is using it, economics, traction, ecosystem and get started.

Open `index.html` with the assets folder beside it. Arrow keys/Page Up/Page Down navigate. S opens sources, T switches day/night, F enters fullscreen. The on-screen controls and horizontal swipes work too. On narrow screens, content reflows for reading.

The narrative prioritizes Amos Meiri’s Antseed interviews and articles. Shahaf’s technical writing and attributed remarks support verification and market integrity. Official product docs are supplements, not misattributed founder writing. See `source-map.json` and `SOURCES.md` for the catalog mapping, selection rationale and access limits.

The input catalog contains 96 records. It has FM IDs rather than the outline’s top-ten ranking; the source panel retains the real catalog IDs. Original evidence paths referenced another computer, and those evidence files were not included in the ZIP. Public article bodies were rechecked for this update. Videos were not watched end-to-end. Sources are limited to Antseed’s own material and posts on X; no outside publications are cited.

The old network-volume and referral slides are replaced by the requested trust and economics story. `evidence-snapshot.json` is retained as a historical record from the previous version and is not cited by the revised narrative.

No fixed price saving, blanket privacy guarantee, guaranteed KBF proof or unconditional buy-and-burn rate is asserted. AIP Final status is distinguished from a deployment audit.

## Brand system

Aligned to the published Antseed v1 guide on 15 November 2026. The unmodified official `brand/tokens.css` and `brand/components.css` provide the shared system; `brand/deck.css` adds the 1280 × 720 presentation layout and mobile reflow. The guide snapshots in `brand/` record the authority used. Network-first wording replaces the old market-led cover. Detailed staking, recognized-usage, buy-and-burn and funding context remain in the relevant source panels.

There are no idle animations or recurring text entrances. Theme changes move through the paired paintings over 1.2 seconds. Reduced motion, `?still` and `?demo` show the finished still immediately. `?theme=light` / `?theme=dark` and the earlier day/night aliases are supported.

Font licensing is included at `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`. Approved logos and paintings retain their original pixels; no new images were generated. The standalone HTML embeds its assets and font license. The source ZIP includes the local fonts, guide snapshots and source map.
