## Selection and limitations

Founder Antseed sources explain strategy. Earlier-career work supports biographies only. Demos support implementation rather than the core narrative. Official Team posts supply product detail and are labelled accordingly. AIP-2 and AIP-4 supply recognized-usage and market-integrity standards; AIP-1 and AIP-6 do not need dedicated slides here. Access-limited AMAs and unlocated replays are excluded. 

Original remote article bodies are not redistributed. The supplied ZIP lists historical evidence paths on another computer; it does not contain those evidence files.

Source policy (15 Nov 2026): only Antseed’s own material (antseed.com docs and blog, AIP standards, the Antseed blog) and posts on X are cited. YouTube, Techzine and Axios were removed. The $2.4M raise is sourced to the Antseed homepage announcement.
